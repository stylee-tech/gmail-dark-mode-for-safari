Get started

  1. Install the app from the Mac App Store and open it.
  2. Click Open Safari Extension Settings. In Safari Settings → Extensions, enable Gmail Dark Mode for Safari.
  3. Allow website access for Gmail, Google Sheets, and Google Search. Safari may also request access to Google account/app-picker frames used inside these sites.
  4. Reload any pages that were already open. Click the extension icon in Safari’s toolbar to choose an appearance.
On — Always dark.
Off — Restore the website’s own appearance.
System — Follow your Mac’s appearance setting.

Each supported product has its own choice. Off does not force a light theme. Preferences are stored on this Mac.

What it changes

Gmail’s inbox, message reading view, and compose window; Google Sheets’ controls and grid; and Google Search’s results and suggestions.

Message foreground and background colors are adjusted together. Photos and image-backed sections retain their original appearance. Some dark logos may remain dark. Sheets’ displayed colors change, while cell values and document formatting remain unchanged.

Requirements

macOS 26 or later and Safari 26 or later. This release supports these three Google products, not every website. A Google account is needed for Gmail and private spreadsheets.

Troubleshooting

If a page stays unchanged, check website access in Safari Settings → Extensions, then reload the page. If the toolbar popup cannot connect, try Retry connection, or open the site in a fresh tab. Use Off for a page whose original appearance you prefer.

To stop using the extension, disable it in Safari Settings → Extensions. Remove the containing app to uninstall it.
