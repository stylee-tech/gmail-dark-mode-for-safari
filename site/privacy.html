Gmail Dark Mode for Safari is provided by Stylee, Inc. This policy explains how the app, extension, and support website handle information.

The app and extension

The app and extension do not collect or transmit browsing history, email contents, spreadsheet data, account details, analytics, or diagnostics. They do not include advertising, trackers, or remote executable code.

The extension works locally. It inspects page elements and their computed colors to change appearance. Gmail message text is not recorded or sent anywhere. Images and image-backed sections keep their original appearance.

Preferences

Your appearance choices are stored in Safari extension-local storage on this device. They are not sent to us or synced through a service operated by us. Choosing Off restores the website’s appearance. Uninstalling the app/extension removes the extension; Safari manages removal of its local data.

Website permissions

Access to Gmail, Google Sheets, and Google Search allows the extension to apply styles. Access to embedded Google account/app-picker frames is used only for supported pages. Standalone Google account pages are not restyled. The extension does not change your account, send mail, or edit spreadsheet values.

This support website

This static site uses no analytics, cookies, or tracking scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, under GitHub’s privacy statement.

When you contact us

If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use that information to answer your request and investigate the issue. Please avoid sending private mailbox or spreadsheet contents. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us.

Other websites and services

Google services and Apple’s App Store operate under their own privacy policies. This extension does not change those services’ data practices.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if our practices change. Questions or deletion requests: hello@trystylee.com.
